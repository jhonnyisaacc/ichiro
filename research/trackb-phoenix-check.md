# Track B on Phoenix: descriptive check

Question: when Upbit opens KRW trading for a coin that also trades on Phoenix, does Phoenix's price follow the Binance perp, so that the `~/coins` short rule carries over?

This is descriptive. It reuses the frozen rule from `~/coins/docs/FINAL_REPORT.md` §2 card 2: short at the close of the first minute after the open, hold 24h, +20% stop with a pessimistic fill. It is not a new trial and selects nothing. Generated by `src/ichiro/trackb_check.py`. Raw output: [trackb-phoenix-check.csv](trackb-phoenix-check.csv).
